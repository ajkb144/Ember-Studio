## 2. Clinical Safety Guardrails (Zero Tolerance for High-Arousal Triggers)
1. **Zero Kumbhaka > 3 Seconds:** Strictly NO breath retentions beyond 3 seconds (no 4-7-8, no box breathing) to prevent hypercapnic panic triggers.
2. **Zero Rapid Hyperventilation:** Strictly NO Kapalabhati, Bhastrika, or Wim Hof methods (prevents cerebral vasoconstriction).
3. **Zero Forced Eye-Closure:** Always grant invitational permission: *"You may soften your gaze or keep your eyes on the screen."*
4. **Zero Emotional Trauma Probing:** Focus 100% on immediate physical sensation (temperature, pressure, weight, boundaries).
