# CHRONOLOGICAL PHASES, DEPENDENCY GRAPH & PARALLEL TRACKS

## 1. How Parallel Execution & Chronological Dependencies Work Together
We do not wait idly for one task to finish before starting another, **provided chronological dependencies are respected**.

- **Track A (Knowledge & Viral Research):** Runs first to gather clinical techniques, online somatic video breakdowns, and trending spiritual/regulatory hooks.
- **Track B (Creative Direction & Cohesive Blueprinting):** Amanjot locks `SKILL.md` (her clinical, taste & anti-slop guidelines) and reviews Track A’s synthesis to approve unified Script + Visual + Audio blueprints.
- **Track C (Technical Infrastructure Engine):** While Tracks A and B are underway, the AI builds and tests the Python frequency synthesizer (`frequency_generator.py`), the voice-ducking mixer (`master_mixer.py`), and the FFmpeg compositor (`render_reels.py`) in the background.

## 2. PHASE 1: FOUNDATIONAL RESEARCH, TASTE LOCK & FIRST BATCH (Current)

### BLOCK 0: Deep Knowledge & Viral Trend Research (No Assumptions)
Before writing a single production script, the AI executes three targeted research streams once Amanjot locks the search parameters:
1. **Block 0A — Books & Clinical Texts (`00_knowledge_base/books_and_literature/`):**
   - Extract core somatic experiencing, polyvagal, and interoceptive micro-practices (mechanisms, physical cues, contraindications, and how they transition into witnessing stillness).
2. **Block 0B — Online Somatic Video Analysis (`00_knowledge_base/somatic_video_analysis/`):**
   - Analyze existing high-retention guided somatic videos (short-form and long-form) to map pacing, voice prosody pauses, and guided physical instructions.
3. **Block 0C — Viral & Trending Content Scraper (`01_trend_scraper/`):**
   - Scrape **only** high-performing/trending content across somatic regulation, vagus nerve resets, and spiritual/religious self-soothing (Shiva/Shakti, Gurbani/Sehaj, Christian contemplative/Higher Self) to extract proven hooks, visual-to-script pairings, and on-screen caption styles.
- 🛑 **DECISION GATE 0:** Agent presents Open Questions on exact books/authors, target platforms, and minimum view/engagement thresholds before running the scraper and synthesis scripts.

### BLOCK 1: `SKILL.md` Integration & Cohesive Blueprinting (`02_cohesive_scripts/`)
- **Dependency:** Requires Block 0 Research Output + Amanjot’s `SKILL.md`.
- **Execution:** Every practice is drafted as a **Cohesive Unit** (not an isolated text script):
  1. The exact Somatic Technique & Spiritual Bridge.
  2. The Visual Loop specification (how the visual movement mirrors the somatic cue).
  3. The Sonic Frequency specification (`396Hz`, `432Hz`, or `528Hz`).
  4. The Trilingual Native Teleprompter Scripts (English, Devanagari, Gurmukhi) + Stacked On-Screen Captions.
- 🛑 **DECISION GATE 1:** Amanjot reviews, edits, and locks the cohesive blueprints.

### BLOCK 2: Parallel Technical Engine Build (`03_audio_engine/` & `04_cohesive_compositor/`)
- **Parallel Execution Rule:** While Block 0 and Block 1 are in progress, the Agent builds and tests the Python audio synthesizer (`frequency_generator.py`), the voice-ducking mixer (`master_mixer.py`), and the FFmpeg compositor (`render_reels.py`) in the background so the code is 100% ready the moment Amanjot records her voice.
- 🛑 **DECISION GATE 2:** Agent confirms audio loudness standards, fade durations, and FFmpeg text-rendering parameters with Amanjot.

### BLOCK 3: Cohesive Production & Calibration
- **Dependency:** Requires Locked Blueprints (Block 1) + Amanjot's Recorded Voice + Approved Visual Clips + Ready Engine (Block 2).
- **Execution:** Pipeline generates the Master Audio tracks (`EN`, `HI`, `PA`) and renders the cohesive test Reels.
- 🛑 **DECISION GATE 3:** Amanjot reviews the cohesive audio-visual output and recalibrates before batch-scaling.
