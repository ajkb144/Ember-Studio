## 1. Executive Summary & Algorithmic Patterns
Across both English and regional South Asian ecosystems (Hindi and Punjabi), short-form wellness algorithms heavily favor **Save Rate (>5–9%)** over superficial comment engagement. ### The Winning 0–3s Hook Framework: "The Somatic Mirror"
- **Mechanic:** The hook names a physical body symptom that the user is currently experiencing unconsciously while scrolling on their phone:
  - *"If your shoulders live up by your ears..."*
  - *"Notice if your tongue is pressed to the roof of your mouth right now."*
  - *"Kya aapka dimaag har waqt kisi anjaane khatre ke intezaar mein rehta hai?"*
  - *"Je tussi har vele andar ik be-chaini mehsus karde ho..."*
- **Algorithmic Effect:** 90%+ retention through 3.0s because the user immediately tests the physical sensation in their own body.
