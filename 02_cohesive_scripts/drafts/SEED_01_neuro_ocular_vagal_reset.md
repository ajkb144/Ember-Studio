## 1. Clinical Mechanism & Sensory Architecture

- **Physiological Action:** Oculomotor-suboccipital neuro-coupling. Shifting gaze $30^\circ$ laterally without rotating the cranium selectively stretches the rectus capitis posterior minor/major and obliquus capitis muscles, decompressing the jugular foramen and triggering ventral vagal motor activity (Cranial Nerves III, IV, VI, X, XI).
- **Clinical Literature Anchor:** Stanley Rosenberg (*Accessing the Healing Power of the Vagus Nerve*).
- **Target Involuntary Marker:** A spontaneous physiological sigh, swallow, gulp, or deep yawn at seconds 20–26.
- **Strict Contraindication Check:** No cervical hyperextension $>30^\circ$. If the viewer has acute cervical disc issues, they keep their head stationary on a flat surface rather than hands.
- **Visual Production Layering:**
  - **Layer 1 (Founder's Painting B-Roll):** 60fps macro footage of Amanjot Kaur's hand sweeping a loaded Chinese calligraphy brush with deep raw indigo pigment across wet heavyweight paper. The brushstroke glides steadily from left to right at a deliberate, soothing cadence.
  - **Layer 2 (Luminous Sacred Geometry):** A fine-line vector geometry overlay (30% opacity, gold/off-white) displaying a central eye horizon line with subtle $30^\circ$ radial alignment arcs, followed by an expanding floral breath mandala that blossoms during the sigh at second 24.
