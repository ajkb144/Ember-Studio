## 1. Stephen W. Porges — Autonomic Hierarchy & Acoustic Neuroception
- **The Social Engagement System (Nucleus Ambiguus):**
  - The ventral vagal complex directly innervates striated muscles of the face, larynx, pharynx, and middle ear (Cranial Nerves V, VII, IX, X, XI).
  - Social safety is perceived acoustically when the middle-ear **stapedius muscle** is tensed, filtering out low-frequency predatory threat rumbles (<85 Hz) and tuning into the human vocal frequency band (**500 Hz to 4,000 Hz**).
- **Audio Engineering Implication for Ember (`03_audio_engine`):**
  - **High-Pass Ambient Soundscape at 85 Hz:** Eliminates subsonic rumbles that unconsciously trigger the dorsal vagal or sympathetic threat neuroception.
  - **Preserve 250 Hz–500 Hz Warm Chest Resonance:** Anchors the soothing parental/ventral vocal tone.
  - **Optimize 1.0 kHz–3.5 kHz Presence:** Keeps vocal consonants legible and intimate without requiring harsh high-frequency amplification.

## 2. Bessel van der Kolk — Anterior Insula & Titrated Containment
- **Anterior Insula Activation (The Body's Internal Map):**
  - Trauma and survival dysregulation disconnect the prefrontal cortex from the anterior insula cortex, creating alexithymia (inability to perceive visceral body signals) or panic overwhelm.
  - Healing requires restoring tactile and visceral contact with the physical container in micro-doses ("titration") without triggering traumatic re-experiencing.
- **Strict Clinical Guardrails for Ember Production:**
  - **No Deep Trauma Probing:** Never ask viewers to "remember when" or "feel into your pain/grief."
  - **Invitational Open-Eye Permission:** Never command viewers to close their eyes (eye closure in hyperarousal can induce vertigo, panic, and dissociation). Always offer: *"You may soften your gaze or rest your eyes on the screen."*
  - **Exteroceptive Anchor First:** Always establish external sensory safety (feet on floor, palms touching sternum) before directing attention to breath or internal organs.
