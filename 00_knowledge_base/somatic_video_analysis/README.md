# Somatic Video Analysis Archive (Block 0B)

This directory stores deep breakdowns of top-performing, high-retention guided somatic practices across YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and long-form guided practices.

## Video Breakdown Schema:
- **Source & Creator:** Handle/Channel, Platform, URL/Identifier
- **Duration & Retention Curve:** Length (seconds), estimated retention drivers
- **Vocal Prosody & Cadence:** Pitch, speed (WPM), pause lengths (seconds) between cues
- **Somatic Action Timing:** Timestamp of cue vs. real-time demonstration
- **Visual Movement & Background:** Color palette, optic flow speed, text placement
- **Key Takeaway for Ember:** What can be adapted into trilingual faceless short-form
